Still carried.

Passed by hand still passing

This coin was struck by hand in the workshop of Andrei of Sibiu, somewhere in the Carpathians where the Dacians once raised wolf banners, from a blank of silver that had already lived one life as a bride's earring. The wolf on its face was cut into the die by an apprentice whose name nobody wrote down, and the strike landed a little off centre, as they often did. It passed through the purse of Ion the shepherd, sewn into a wedding shirt in Maramureș, and was hidden beneath a church floor for two hundred years. A weaver's granddaughter, Ileana Popescu, found it in the spring of 1918 and kept it in her dowry chest under embroidered linen. The high points wore smooth against her thumb. Every scratch on it is a hand it has known, and every hand left something behind for the next.
